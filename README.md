# Executive Compensation & CEO Salary Analytics Dashboard

## Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing executive compensation structures, CEO pay multiples relative to median worker pay, top executive earners, and industry distribution across major corporate sectors.

## Key Analytical Insights
* **Industry CEO Pay Multiples:** Evaluates average CEO-to-worker pay ratios across sectors including Consumer Discretionary, Staples, and Information Technology.
* **Worker Pay vs. CEO Salary:** Compares average median worker salaries against average CEO total compensation by industry sector.
* **Top Executive Earners:** Ranks the Top 10 highest-paid CEOs along with total annual salary metrics.
* **Sector Distribution:** Tracks the total number of companies represented across major sectors (Financials, Industrials, Health Care, Tech, Real Estate).

## Tools & Technologies
* **Data Visualization:** Power BI Desktop (Bar & Column Charts, Ranking Tables, Dual Metrics)
* **Data Transformation:** Power Query ETL Workflows
* **Reporting:** Executive dashboard design & report exporting
